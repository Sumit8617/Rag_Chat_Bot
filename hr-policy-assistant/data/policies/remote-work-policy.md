# Remote Work Policy

**Document title:** Remote Work Policy
**Version:** 1.0
**Applies to:** Full-time employees in India
**Owner:** Human Resources / Workplace Operations

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines eligibility, equipment support, and expectations for employees working outside a company office.

## 2. Eligibility tiers

Remote work eligibility and equipment allowance depend on role tier, as set by the employee's manager at the start of each year.

| Tier | Remote days per week | One-time equipment allowance | Internet reimbursement (monthly) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fully Remote | 5 | ₹40,000 | ₹1,500 |
| Hybrid | 3 | ₹25,000 | ₹1,000 |
| Office-First | 1 | ₹10,000 | Not covered |

Equipment allowance may be used for a monitor, chair, or keyboard/mouse, and requires a receipt submitted within 60 days of purchase.

## 3. Core hours

All employees, regardless of tier, must be reachable on chat between **11:00 AM and 4:00 PM IST** on working days, unless on approved leave.

## 4. Requesting a tier change

Employees may request a change in remote work tier once every 6 months by submitting a request to their manager. Manager approval is required; HR approval is required only if the change affects the equipment allowance already disbursed that year.

## 5. What this policy does not cover

This policy does not cover visa sponsorship for remote work from another country, co-working space stipends, or relocation assistance. Those, if applicable, are handled through separate HR processes.
